This is a small software company being built around problems we know firsthand—and products we would actually want to use.

WHY THIS EXISTSBuilt from operations.

Not from a boardroom theory of how operations should work.

Software shaped by the people who actually have to use it.

JELIX started from a simple frustration: the people responsible for complex facilities, projects and physical assets are often surrounded by software, yet still depend on spreadsheets, disconnected systems and institutional memory to understand what is really happening.

The company is being built by an operations and automation professional with firsthand experience managing maintenance, SCADA, contractors, infrastructure projects and the daily realities of keeping critical physical systems running. JWorks and TRACE grew from those problems—not from a search for a marketable software category.

We're building slowly and deliberately: solve a real problem, use the product, learn from it, then earn the right to expand.

Built for the floor, not the demo.Useful during real work matters more than impressive feature lists.
Reduce administration.Software should remove repetitive work, not create another system to feed.
Preserve context.Decisions, changes and asset history should survive staff turnover.
Connect what already exists.Controls, CMMS and business systems should become more useful together.
